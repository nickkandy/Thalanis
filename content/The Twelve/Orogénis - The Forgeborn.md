#God - #Thalanis #Light #Fire
### Ορογένης - ο Σφυρηλατημένος
**Domains:** Craft, Fire, Creation
**Manifestation:** Pride, Loyalty
**Symbol:** A hammer striking an anvil of stars  
**Creed:** “From form comes purpose.”  
**Allies:** [[Eluna - The Smiling Tide]], [[Kyrthοs - The Tempest King]]
**Rival:** [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Kaelion hammered the bones of the earth. He shaped mountains, steel, and the first dwarves. His worshippers see labor as sanctity, every act of making echoes the divine forge.

Wedded to [[Eluna - The Smiling Tide|Serelune]], who tends to the snows on his peaks and the rivers that flow from his mountains, ever in love with the world they shaped.

In his pride, [[Orogénis - The Forgeborn|Kaelion]] sought to forge a perfect soul. [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal|Kharanox]] arose from that flame, burning with ambition and hunger.
