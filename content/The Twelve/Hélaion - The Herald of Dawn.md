#God - #Thalanis  #Light
### Ήλαιων - Ο Κήρυκας της Αυγής
**Domains:** Light, Hope, Purity  
**Manifestation:** Justice, Hopefulness
**Creed:** “Every dawn demands the death of night.”  
**Allies:** [[Opsiom - The Father of Logos]], [[Kyrthοs - The Tempest King]]  
**Rival:** [[Lythix - The Veiled Fate]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Helimir was first to draw #Light upon the void, banishing the dark of uncreation. His radiance defines day, justice, and renewal of the spirit. The one who trigger the first **Dawn**, when **time** itself began, the **First Hour**.
