### Νηρηθέα - η Βυθισμένη Βασίλισσα
**Domains:** Water, Death, Memory
**Manifestation:** Sorrow, Grief
**Symbol:** A crown of coral over dark waves  
**Creed:** “All that sinks shall rise again.”  
**Allies:** [[Eluna - The Smiling Tide]], [[Kyrthοs - The Tempest King]]
**Rival:** None

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Nerithys claimed the deep and the forgotten. She rules the depths where light cannot reach. She embodies the sea’s mercurial grace, loving, wrathful, and ever-changing. Sailors pray to her to keep the creatures that live in her darκ abbys tranquil. Her followers say the ocean remembers everything, even the gods’ sins.

Twin sister of [[Eluna - The Smiling Tide|Elune]]

[[Kyrthοs - The Tempest King]], enamored with [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen|Nerithys]] sought to rise with her into the heights of heaven. As she slipped from his grasp and fell, she descended into the deep, cold waters, into the eternal domain of the [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen|Drowned Queen]].
