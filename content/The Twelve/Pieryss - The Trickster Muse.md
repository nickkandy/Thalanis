#God - #Feydance #Nature 
### Πιερίς - Το Παιγνιώδες Πνεύμα
**Domains:** Chaos, Inspiration, Freedom
**Manifestation:** Playfulness, Intimacy
**Symbol:** A mask of mirrored glass  
**Creed:** “Creation without chaos is a cage.”  
**Allies:** [[Myrvéth- The Spellweaver]], [[Anthea - The Verdant Mother]]
**Rival:** [[Syptéa - The Blighted Maiden]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Together with [[Myrvéth- The Spellweaver]] they danced the [[Feydance]] into existence.

[[Pieryss - The Trickster Muse]] scattered chaos and inspiration. It dances through mortal hearts as the spark of creativity that births art and rebellion. It’s followers believe truth is found only through contradiction.

Together with [[Myrvéth- The Spellweaver]] they created all the magical creatures that live in the [[Feydance]], including the **Elves**
