#God - #Thalanis #Arcana
### Ώψιομ - Ο Αρχέγονος Λόγος
**Domains:** Knowledge, Law, Order
**Manifestation:** Knowledge, Trust
**Symbol:** A radiant sunburst inscribed with runes  
**Creed:** “Truth is the first flame.”  
**Allies:** [[Anthea - The Verdant Mother]], [[Hélaion - The Herald of Dawn]], [[Myrvéth- The Spellweaver]]
**Rival:** [[Lythix - The Veiled Fate]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

The architect of logic and language, Aurelian gave structure to the divine song. Scholars call his words “The First Grammar”: the laws upon which reality was written.

Wedded to [[Anthea - The Verdant Mother]], together they gave life to everything that breaths in [[Thālanis]].
