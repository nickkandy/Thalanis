#God - #Feydance #Arcana 
### Μυρβέθ - Το Υφάνων της Μαγείας
**Domains:** Magic, Dreams, Transformation
**Manifestation:** Wonder, Curiosity  
**Creed:** “All that is imagined, is.”  
**Allies:** [[Pieryss - The Trickster Muse]], [[Opsiom - The Father of Logos]]
**Rival:** [[Lythix - The Veiled Fate]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Together with [[Pieryss - The Trickster Muse]] they danced the [[Feydance]] into existence..

Myreth taught creation how to dream. It wove magic into existence, the hidden fabric that bridges the arcane and the tangible. It granted the mortals **Magic**, #Arcana made gentle and wieldable. Dreamers, mages, and visionaries call her the Keeper of Dreams.

Together with [[Pieryss - The Trickster Muse]] they created all the magical creatures that live in the [[Feydance]], including the **Elves**
