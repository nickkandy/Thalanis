#God - #Thalanis #Water 
### Ελούνα - Η Χαμογελαστή Παλίρροια
**Domains:** Change, Luck, Joy  
**Manifestation:** Joy, Beauty
**Symbol:** A laughing moon over rippling water
**Creed:** “Flow with joy, and the world will follow.”  
**Allies:** [[Orogénis - The Forgeborn]], [[Anthea - The Verdant Mother]], [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen]]
**Rival:** [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Twin sister of [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen]]

Serelune dances in the waters that meet the land, rivers that wander, lakes that mirror the sky, and shores kissed by the turning tides of her lost sister. She embodies the sea’s gentler face: its laughter upon the beaches, its playfulness in falling snow, its beauty shimmering under moonlight. Her presence brings celebration and serenity wherever waters touch the world. Those who honor her believe that joy is like water — meant to flow freely, shaping even the hardest stone not through force but through grace.

As she wept for her lost sister, [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen]], she gave birth to the **Moon** , forever reflecting upon the waves, a silent beacon for the lost.

Wedded to [[Orogénis - The Forgeborn]] together forging land and stone, ever in love with the world they shaped.
