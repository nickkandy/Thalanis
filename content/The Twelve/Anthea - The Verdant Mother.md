#God - #Thalanis #Nature
### Ανθέα - η Χλοερή Μητέρα
**Domains:** Life, Nature, Renewal
**Manifestation:** Peacefulness, Love
**Depiction:** A matron cloaked in vines and light, eyes of green-gold.  
**Allies:** [[Opsiom - The Father of Logos]], [[Eluna - The Smiling Tide]], [[Pieryss - The Trickster Muse]]
**Rival:** [[Syptéa - The Blighted Maiden]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Elyndra’s breath is the cycle of life itself. Her priests tend sacred groves and believe that to cultivate is to honor her eternal promise, that no death is final while the world grows green.

Wedded to [[Opsiom - The Father of Logos]], together they gave life to everything that breaths in [[Thālanis]].
