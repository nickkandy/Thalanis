#God - #Shadowvail #Nature
### Σηπτεία - Η Μαραμένη Κόρη
**Domains:** Decay, Suffering, Renewal
**Manifestation:** Disgust, Disease
**Symbol:** A wilted rose crowned in thorns  
**Creed:** “To end is to begin.”  
**Allies:** [[Lythix - The Veiled Fate]]  
**Rival:** [[Anthea - The Verdant Mother]], [[Pieryss - The Trickster Muse]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Together with [[Lythix - The Veiled Fate]] they created the [[Shadowvail]].

Illirth shaped decay so that life could end and begin anew. She personifies necessary decay, the rot that feeds the soil. She seized the opportunity of *mortal death* and established the circle of life and rebirth through decomposition. Her priests perform funerary rites and see beauty in the unraveling of all things.
