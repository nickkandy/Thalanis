#God - #Heavens #Nature #Water
### Κύρθως - ο Βασιλιάς της Θύελλας
**Domains:** Storms, Freedom, Air
**Manifestation:** Anger, Powerfulness
**Symbol:** A lightning bolt through a broken crown  
**Creed:** “No chain endures the storm.”  
**Allies:** [[Orogénis - The Forgeborn]], [[Hélaion - The Herald of Dawn]], [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen]]
**Rival:** [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Celandor breathed storm and motion into the air. In his fury for loosing [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen]] he unleashed the first **Storm**, and the world trembled beneath it. He is the tempest’s roar, both liberator and destroyer. His followers honor him through acts of daring and rebellion against the static and the still.

Creator of the [[Celestial Heavens]] to contradict [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal|Kharanox]]'s [[Nine Hells]] to welcome the souls of the righteous.

Enamored with [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen|Nerithys]], he sought to rise with her into the heights of heaven. As she slipped from his grasp and fell, she descended into the deep, cold waters, into the eternal domain of the [[Nerithys - The Drowned Queen|Drowned Queen]].
