### Lήθηξ - Η Πεπλοφόρος Μοίρα
**Domains:** Fate, Death, Secrets
**Manifestation:** Death, Despair
**Creed:** “All paths lead home.”  
**Allies:** [[Syptéa - The Blighted Maiden]]
**Rival:** [[Opsiom - The Father of Logos]], [[Hélaion - The Herald of Dawn]], [[Myrvéth- The Spellweaver]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

Together with [[Syptéa - The Blighted Maiden]] they created the [[Shadowvail]].

Lythix knows every destiny, yet never speaks it. In her spite, gave to the races a terrible gift: **mortality**. Her followers walk the line between prophecy and doom, trusting that every end is a necessary silence.
