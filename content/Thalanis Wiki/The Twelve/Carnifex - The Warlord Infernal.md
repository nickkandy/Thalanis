#God - #Hells #Fire
### Κάρνιφεξ - Ο Άρχων της Φλόγας
**Domains:** War, Ambition, Fire
**Manifestation:** Fear, Aggression
**Depiction:** A horned (sometimes) warlord apparating from fire
**Allies:** None truly trusted  
**Rival:** [[Orogénis - The Forgeborn]], [[Kyrthοs - The Tempest King]], [[Eluna - The Smiling Tide]]

One of the [[The Twelve of Creation]]

[[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal]], forged conflict, and passion, the fire in mortal hearts. The embodiment of defiance and conquest, [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal|Carnifex]] sees conflict as sacred evolution, the trial by which the strong ascend and the weak are reforged.

In his pride, [[Orogénis - The Forgeborn|Orogénis]] sought to forge a perfect soul. [[Carnifex - The Warlord Infernal|Carnifex]] arose from that flame, burning with ambition and hunger.
