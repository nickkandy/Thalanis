Medium sized town on the South East coast of the continent of [[Starting Continent]], near the mouth of the river [[Southern River]]. Due to its geography near the river waters, the town has a big deposit of underground drinking water. It's the last sizable town before the border with the dry-lands of [[The Neck]]. In the [[Wars of the Shattered Veil]] it was the first settlement that attacking fiends and devils met while marching from the south. For this reason the Western side of the town is heavily reinforced.
